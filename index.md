My name is **Colleen Nugent McLean** and I am the Editor of [Digital Humanities Now](https://digitalhumanitiesnow.org/) and the [Centers for Digital Scholarship](https://cds.library.northeastern.edu/) Coordinator.

I have my PhD in World History from Northeastern University. I have Graduate Certificates in Digital Humanities and Public History. My research focuses on British colonialism, anti-Muslim rhetoric, digital humanities methods and pedagogy, and computational text analysis. 

My dissertation, "Mussulman Fanatics" A Computational Analysis of Anti-Muslim Racism in *The Times* from the 1857 Sepoy Rebellion to the Rushdie Affair, uses digital methods to chart the construction of modern British anti-Muslim racism, specifically the idea that Islam is inherently ‘fanatical’ and ‘violent.’ Using case studies on the 1857 Rebellion in India, 1898 Battle of Omdurman in the Sudan, and the German-Ottoman declaration of jihad during the First World War, my dissertation argues that the contours of language around Islam in the colonial period provided much of the language that influences modern debates in the late 1980s through the present. To do so, I use word embedding models on *The Times* of London. To read more or explore these word embedding models, see the [companion website to the dissertation](https://clnugentmclean.github.io/dissertation/).
